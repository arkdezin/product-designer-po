# Executive Brief / Problem Intake Note

## Discovery
This brief came in from the product manager and the data analytics/data engineering team because they are facing some issues with the data quality that is being captured in the catalog. We needed a solution for this one for our internal team so that they can have Unified and centralized workflow.

## Problem Reported
The problem reported was that, since this whole workflow required multiple teams' involvement at multiple stages, there was no centralized or common workflow or a platform where things could go without any discrepancies. Due to this scattered and ununiformed workflow, there were a lot of data discrepancies and client dissatisfaction, which led to a a lot of support  tickets for data quality and a lot of manual intervention and correction happening on the catalog. 

## Business Goal
The business goal was to solve this problem and make it so that the data quality improves. There are a few things that we were aiming for in the business goal here:
1. The quality of data increases.
2. The turnaround time of data from start to end is reduced.
3. Making sure that the teams are not doing a lot of manual work, which leads to productivity issues and efficiency.

## Initial Constraints
Since there were multiple teams that needed to be working on the platform, some basic things like:
- user role definition
- different data visibility to different team members
- submitting that data for a review purpose

## Target Audience
The target audience was our internal team, including:
- business management, 
- product management, 
- data engineering & analytics, and 
- the data quality team.

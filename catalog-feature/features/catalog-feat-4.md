# Catalog Maintenance Product Review (Validation)

## Description

Provide Internal Teams with a centralized workflow for reviewing, cleaning, correcting and approving syndicated products.

## Benefit Hypothesis

A centralized workflow for internal teams will improve in Data quality score, increase trust on catalog, reduce manual workflow and reduced operational cost

## Acceptance Criteria

- When accessing product review screen, the team members should have following:
  - Raw Data
  - AI Enriched/Enhanced Data
  - Final Data
    - By Default all fields from the raw data should be deemed as final data.
- On visit to product review screen, these are capabilities based of user roles:
  - Product review team members have capability to validate and send the product for level 2 approval.
  - DM team will be able to approve the products send for approval by the PR team
  - Other teams have similar capabilities as Product review team
- When choosing data from Raw or AI enriched, user should be able to mark confidence, add need clarification statement if not sure
- Following are the actions available to the users:
  - back to queue
  - drafts
  - send for approval (Product Review team) and Approve product (DM team)
  - next product

# Catalog Maintenance Dashboard

## Description

Provide Internal Teams with a centralized workflow for reviewing, cleaning, correcting and approving syndicated products.

## Benefit Hypothesis

A centralized workflow for internal teams will improve in Data quality score, increase trust on catalog, reduce manual workflow and reduced operational cost

## Acceptance Criteria

- When Internal Teams, other than Product Review (Validation), logs in, the admin portal will have a option to access catalog maintenance.
- When accessing Maintenance Dashboard, the team members should have following:
  - Quick Stats
  - Filters
  - New view/workspace
  - Grid with Product details
  - Column settings
  - CTA to navigate to Pending products
- when accessed the product for actions(CTA), there should be option to edit/modify

## A centralized platform for reviewing, cleaning, correcting & approving syndicated product

## Description

For Internal Teams who need a simpler and effective way to communicate and work, the Catalog Maintenance is a solution that centralizes workflow for reviewing, cleaning, and approving new syndicate data and correcting the issues in the existing catalog data that helps addresses lack of proper process & a clear path, currently data management, data scrapping, product management and business teams rely on manual, fragmented processes, leading to inconsistent product data quality, higher operational effort, and reduced trust in the catalog unlike manual data reviewing, cleaning and correcting.

## Benefit Hypothesis

We believe that building a centralized workflow for internal teams to review, clean, approve new syndicated products and correcting issues in existing products will increase in Catalog Quality Score, increased trust on catalog list, Increase in operational efficiency and reduction in manual communication and workflow

## Business Outcome

A Centralized platform with clear workflow

**Will result in:**

- Increase in Data Quality/Confidence Score by 5-10%
- Increased Trust on Catalog Products by 5%
- Reduction in manual workflow by 15-20%
- Better feedback resulting in 3-5% improved catalog and reduced operational effort by 10-15%

## Leading Indicators

- Increased efficiency of the internal teams by 10-20%
- Reduction in the time taken to process weekly data by 5-10%
- Early feedback will result in identification of defective data in catalog, increasing catalog health by 10-15%.

# Catalog Maintenance Queue

## Description

Provide Internal Teams with a centralized workflow for reviewing, cleaning, correcting and approving syndicated products.

## Benefit Hypothesis

A centralized workflow for internal teams will improve in Data quality score, increase trust on catalog, reduce manual workflow and reduced operational cost

## Acceptance Criteria

- All team member have access to this module.
- Internal team other than Product Review (Validation), should have navigation access from the dashboard screen to this module.
- When Product review team login to catalog maintenance, this will be the default and first screen they navigate.
- When users first see the screen, the team members should have following:
  - filters to drill down results
  - grid/table view to see the list of product pending review/need clarification/modification etc.
- Team manager should be able to choose/select products and assign the products to team members for validation/verification
- All team members should have ability to navigate to the product review screen

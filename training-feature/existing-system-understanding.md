# Existing System Understanding

After the executive brief was given, there was an initial meeting set up with the field ops team managers who create the training to better understand:

- their current existing day-to-day training creation and assignment flows
- what they do currently
- what problems they are facing with that
- what the actual requirement is that we need to build this training module on

So in the initial call the field ops team showed us the third-party platform that they are currently using, which is **“Litmos”**, and they gave us some key points in that initial meeting. Starting with the first point, all the training created on that platform can be assigned to individual users using email ID.
The user will receive an email on their email relating to that training and they need to complete that training on a web, basically either on a laptop or on a desktop device. There was no mobile responsive view or basically there was no way for the research associate to take the training on a mobile device so that was one of the key points that they mentioned while talking to us.

Second they showed what current capabilities they have in the third-party platform. There were two different ways that the trainings could be created and assigned to the Research Associates or RA:

1. One way was individual training, where you create a single training with some questions in it and assign it to some people.
2. The second way was that you can create a learning path and you can add as much training within that learning path to create a course kind of thing instead of just a single training. Maybe let's say, for example, there is a learning path called “on-boarding”. If whenever a new RA is joining the team, they can assign that learning path of “on-boarding” and let's say there are ten different trainings in that learning path. That gives the RA the whole idea from A to Z that every RA should know with that learning path.

The next thing was that while talking to them, we asked what type of training materials or questions they ask in those training sessions. They told us that they have a video and some questions based on that video, and the questions are multiple-choice questions. 

After further discussion with the Field Ops team, we identified that these are not like training that have grades, or certificates or things like that.

 
These trainings are more like guidance rather than a capability-testing training where you grade someone on the capability. These trainings are more like guidance for the RA: if you have to perform a certain job, what is the best way that you can perform that job really well?

And there were some explicit requirements from the field of steam that they currently were using on the **“Litmos”** platform, which was that the videos cannot be fast forwarded or skipped. They have to watch the complete video.
